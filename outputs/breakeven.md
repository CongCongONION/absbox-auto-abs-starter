# Break-even CNL (CDR multiplier on base segment CDRs; CPR / recovery / lag at base)

Bisection tolerance 0.005x on the multiplier. Each tranche reports a BRACKET: at the lower bound the tranche is still whole, at the upper bound it has its first (small) loss; the break-even CNL lies between the two CNL values.

- Class E: break-even CDR multiplier in [3.844x, 3.847x]; break-even CNL in [$8,630,114 (8.63%), $8,635,513 (8.64%)] of the pool (loss at lower bound $0; at upper bound $4,150)
- Class B: break-even CDR multiplier in [5.635x, 5.639x]; break-even CNL in [$12,053,784 (12.05%), $12,060,948 (12.06%)] of the pool (loss at lower bound $0; at upper bound $9,973)
